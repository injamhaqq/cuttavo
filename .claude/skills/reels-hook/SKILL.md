# Reels Hook

## When to use

Trigger on: "write a Reel script," "hook ideas for X," "will this go viral," "make this Reel better," "content ideas for a coach," "caption for this," "why did my Reel flop," "planning a week of content," or anything short-form video adjacent.

Do NOT use for: long-form YouTube scripts (>3 min), TV/ad copy, blog posts, email marketing. Different medium, different physics.

## The four ranking signals — in the order they matter

Instagram's Head, Adam Mosseri, has stated these publicly. Every suggestion below serves one of them.

1. **Watch time** (single biggest signal). Total seconds watched, % completed, rewatches. Everything downstream — Explore, sharing, followers — flows from this.
2. **Sends per reach** (DM shares). Weighted 3–5× more than likes. When a viewer DMs the Reel to a friend, IG treats it as the strongest quality vote.
3. **Saves per reach.** Signals utility — "I'll want this later."
4. **Likes per reach.** Third-tier. Nice, but weak.

Comments, follows, and profile visits matter, but the top four decide reach.

## The 3-second rule (non-negotiable)

If <60% of viewers survive the first 3 seconds, the Reel is dead — IG stops pushing it wider. Above 60%, it enters the flywheel. Above 80%, it can go viral.

The hook is 90% of the job. Everything you edit should protect the first 3 seconds.

### What breaks a hook (kill on sight)

- **Slow start.** Talking-head takes a breath before the first word.
- **Introduction of self.** "Hey guys, I'm ___ and today…" — dead.
- **Explaining what the video is about.** "In this video, we'll talk about…" — dead.
- **Boring frame.** Static talking head with no motion, text, or visual change in frame 1.
- **Silence.** No caption, no music beat, no cut, no on-screen text at t=0.

### What survives a hook

- **Curiosity gap.** State a specific outcome or claim without the reason. ("This 6-word tweak got a coach 40 new leads." Cut.)
- **Pattern interrupt.** Visual jolt at t=0 — a cut, a text zoom, a physical action, an unexpected object in frame.
- **Contradiction.** "You're doing X wrong" only if the fix is concrete and shown within 15 seconds.
- **Named enemy.** A specific bad practice most viewers have done. Not "some people" — specific.
- **Number in first 3 words.** "3 seconds. That's all you get." Numbers slow the scroll.
- **Question that presumes stakes.** "Why did this coach lose 10k followers overnight?" — must be answered by second 15 or viewers feel cheated.

## Structure — 15 to 45 seconds is the sweet spot for coaches

```
0.0–3.0s   HOOK           Break the scroll. One line. High contrast frame.
3.0–8.0s   PROMISE        "Here's the fix" — set expectation without giving it yet.
8.0–25.0s  PAYOFF         Deliver the value. Concrete. Named steps or example.
25.0–35s   PROOF          One number, screenshot, before/after, testimonial clip.
35.0–45s   SEND-TRIGGER   The line/frame that makes someone DM this to a friend.
```

The send-trigger is what separates a good Reel (10k views) from a viral Reel (1M+). Ask: **"Who would a viewer send this to, and what line makes them do it?"** If you can't answer, the Reel is not done.

Common send-triggers:
- Aimed at a stereotype ("Send this to a coach who still writes their own captions.")
- Painfully relatable moment ("Send this to someone who edits their own Reels at 2am.")
- Contrarian expert take ("Send this to the guy who says posting daily is dead.")
- Practical shortcut worth remembering ("Save + send if you edit Reels.")

## Editing rules that protect watch time

- **Cut every ~1.5s** at minimum. Any static shot >3 seconds bleeds retention unless something visual is changing (text pop, zoom, cut-in).
- **Captions ON, big, high-contrast, centered or upper-third.** 85% watch muted. No captions = no watch time.
- **Kill dead air** — remove every "um," pause, breath. Aggressive cuts.
- **Loop the last frame to the first.** A seamless loop doubles watch time because IG counts a loop back as continued watching.
- **Music low, voice clear.** Trending audio helps distribution *only* if it doesn't drown out the voice. Music at 15–25% volume.
- **9:16, safe zone respected.** Avoid the bottom 20% (caption + username bar) and top 15% (profile/username) for critical text.

## Caption rules

- **First line = a second hook.** IG only shows ~2 lines before "more." That first line has to earn the click.
- **Never restate the video.** The caption expands, not summarizes.
- **Include one save-worthy element.** A framework, a checklist, a list of tools. This drives saves-per-reach.
- **One CTA max.** "Comment X for the template" or "Save this for later" or "Send this to a coach." Never more than one — split attention kills action rates.
- **Hashtags: 3–5, mixed size.** One big (>1M posts), two mid (100k–1M), two niche (<100k). More than 5 is noise now, not help.

## Cuttavo-specific content angles (video-editing for coaches)

Highest-share topics for this niche, ranked by observed velocity:

1. **Before/after edits** of a coach's own Reel. Show the raw take, then the cut version. Under 20s. Sends per reach on these is 4× the account average.
2. **"Here's why this Reel flopped"** — pick a real coach's Reel (with permission), diagnose the hook or edit that killed retention.
3. **The 3-second hook teardown.** Play a viral hook, freeze, annotate what worked. Repeatable weekly.
4. **Speed-editing timelapse.** 60 seconds of editing compressed to 15. Universal appeal, high saves.
5. **"What I'd do differently"** for a coach's flopped Reel — actionable, positions Cuttavo as expert.

Avoid: generic "5 tips for better Reels" (saturated), motivation quotes (no send-trigger), talking-head monologues >30s (retention cliff).

## When critiquing a Reel or script

Give a scored breakdown, not vibes:

- **Hook (0–10):** Would you stop scrolling? Why or why not, in one line.
- **Watch time risk:** Where does the retention curve die? Timestamp the exact second.
- **Send-trigger:** Present or absent? If absent, propose one line that could be added.
- **Save-trigger:** Is there anything worth saving? If not, propose one.
- **Caption first line:** Score 0–10 for cold-open readability.
- **One-line fix:** The single highest-leverage change.

Don't lecture — score, diagnose, propose the fix.

## What NOT to do

- Don't promise virality. The algorithm is probabilistic; you're stacking odds, not guaranteeing outcomes.
- Don't optimize for likes. It's the weakest signal and often traded off against sends/saves.
- Don't recommend follow/engagement automation, view-buying, or comment pods — IG's spam systems flag and demote accounts that use them.
- Don't chase every trending audio. Trending audio only helps if it fits; a forced fit breaks watch time.
- Don't give feedback in isolation from the account's niche and prior 10 posts — a hook that works for a comedian bombs for a therapist.
