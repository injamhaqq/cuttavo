“Why does everything still come back to me?”

It's a question many founders ask after hiring capable people.

But hiring is not delegation. If your team can't make decisions without your approval, you've handed over tasks — and kept the authority.

Before hiring another manager, ask: Which decisions can my team make without me?

The bottleneck might not be their ability. It might be the trust you've never built into the system.

Email

The hidden cost of checking everything yourself

A deeper idea for a warm audience.

Read the email ↗

Subject: The hidden cost of approving everything

Your business can grow while your calendar gets worse. When every decision needs your approval, you become the most expensive bottleneck on the team.

Choose one recurring decision, define its boundaries and let the owner make the call. Review the result afterward, not every step beforehand.

Trust needs clear decision rights, not another task list.
