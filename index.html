Fit matters

Built for experts whose knowledge is connected to revenue.

Cuttavo is intentionally narrower than a generic content agency. The economics work best when one new customer is meaningfully valuable to your business.

Best fit

  • Business and executive coaches with an established offer
  • Consultants, advisors and fractional executives
  • B2B founders selling expertise-heavy products or services
  • Speakers and subject-matter experts with existing IP
  • Experts with calls, podcasts, webinars or ideas already being underused

Probably not a fit

  • Someone looking only for the cheapest editor
  • An aspiring creator without a clear offer or expertise
  • A brand that wants generic AI content at maximum volume
  • A business expecting guaranteed virality or guaranteed customers
  • Anyone unwilling to contribute real ideas, experience or point of view
