Who this is built for

Experts with something valuable to sell — and no desire to become full-time creators.

We are deliberately not trying to serve everybody who wants social media content.

Strong fit

  • Business, executive and specialist coaches with an established offer
  • Consultants, advisors and fractional executives
  • Speakers, podcast hosts and subject-matter experts
  • B2B founders whose expertise helps sell the company
  • People already producing ideas but using only a fraction of them

Not what Cuttavo is for

  • Someone shopping only for the cheapest video editor
  • Beginners without a clear offer, audience or expertise yet
  • Brands wanting hundreds of generic AI posts
