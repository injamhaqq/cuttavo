Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cuttavo collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, use Cuttavo services, or connect an Instagram professional account to Cuttavo.

Effective: August 17, 2026Contact: admin@cuttavo.studio

1. Who we are

Cuttavo ("Cuttavo," "we," "us," or "our") provides video-editing and related content services, together with software and automation tools that may help customers manage Instagram conversations, contacts, approvals, bookings, and related workflows.

Questions about this policy or requests relating to personal information can be sent to admin@cuttavo.studio.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use Cuttavo, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account and profile information: names, usernames, Instagram professional account identifiers, profile details, and account connection status.
  • Instagram messaging and interaction data: messages sent to or from a connected Instagram professional account, message identifiers, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, webhook events, and related interaction metadata that Meta makes available to the app.
  • Content and business information: information you choose to provide about your business, content needs, services, preferences, qualification details, or other material you submit to Cuttavo.
  • Authentication data: OAuth access tokens and related authorization metadata required to keep a connected Instagram account authenticated. Access tokens are kept server-side and are not displayed publicly.
  • Contact and booking information: email addresses, scheduling details, booking status, and other information supplied during a conversation or booking workflow.
  • Technical and operational information: IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, request metadata, application logs, error information, security events, and service usage information needed to operate and protect the service.

3. How we use information

We use information only for legitimate service, operational, security, and support purposes, including to:

  • connect and authenticate Instagram professional accounts;
  • receive and process Instagram webhook events and messages;
  • display conversations, contacts, approvals, booking states, and related account activity inside Cuttavo;
  • prepare, review, and where authorized, send replies through Instagram;
  • provide AI-assisted drafting or workflow support when those features are enabled;
  • provide video-editing and related services requested by customers;
  • detect abuse, secure accounts, prevent unauthorized access, troubleshoot failures, and maintain reliability;
  • respond to support, privacy, and data-deletion requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.
Cuttavo does not sell Instagram messaging data or connected-account access tokens.

4. Instagram and Meta data

When you connect an Instagram professional account, Cuttavo uses Meta's Instagram APIs and OAuth flow to request only the permissions needed for the enabled features. Meta controls what information its APIs make available and may apply its own terms, privacy practices, permission rules, and retention requirements.

Cuttavo uses Instagram data to provide the features requested by the account owner, such as account connection, messaging, conversation handling, approvals, and related workflow functions. We do not use Instagram data to build unrelated advertising profiles.

5. AI-assisted processing

If AI-assisted drafting or analysis is enabled, relevant message text, business context, or conversation information may be sent to an AI service provider solely to generate the requested draft, classification, summary, or workflow output. We limit this processing to what is reasonably necessary for the enabled feature.

6. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Cuttavo, such as hosting, database, infrastructure, messaging/API, AI, scheduling, monitoring, analytics, and security providers. They may process information only as needed to provide those services to us.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights or safety of Cuttavo or others, to investigate fraud or abuse, or in connection with a corporate transaction such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and conversation history, satisfy operational or security needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

When an Instagram account is disconnected, we stop using its access token for future API access. Certain records may remain for a limited period in operational systems or backups unless earlier deletion is required by law or requested and reasonably possible.

8. Data deletion and account disconnection

You can disconnect an Instagram account from Cuttavo through the applicable integration controls. To request deletion of personal information associated with Cuttavo, email admin@cuttavo.studio with the subject "Cuttavo Data Deletion Request" and identify the account or information involved.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify the requester before completing a deletion request. We will delete or de-identify information that we are required or able to remove, subject to legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, and backup-retention needs.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. These measures include restricting access to sensitive credentials and keeping OAuth tokens server-side. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing

Cuttavo and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a copy of certain personal information. You may also withdraw a connected Instagram account's authorization by disconnecting the integration or through the relevant Meta/Instagram account settings.

To exercise a privacy right, contact admin@cuttavo.studio.

12. Children's privacy

Cuttavo is intended for businesses and professional users and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the service.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Cuttavo, our integrations, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated policy at this URL and update the effective date above when material changes are made.
